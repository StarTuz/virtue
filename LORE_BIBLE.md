# Veridia Lore & World Bible

## 1. The World: Veridia
Veridia is a realm of high fantasy where the physical reality is shaped by the metaphysical alignment of its inhabitants. It is a world of floating islands and fractured continents, held together by the **Ley-Weave**.

### 1.1 The History
The world was once whole, known as "The Prime". But the **Great Discordance** shattered the land, separating communities by vast voids of magical static. Only by mastering the Virtues can the Paragons knit the world back together.

## 2. The Three Foundations (Principles)
Unlike the classic axioms, Veridia is built upon:
1.  **Insight** (The Mind) - To see the world as it truly is.
2.  **Sympathy** (The Heart) - To feel the connection between all living things.
3.  **Tenacity** (The Will) - To endure and act despite adversity.

## 3. The Eight Tenets (Virtues)
Derived from the combinations of the Foundations:

| Tenet | Derivation | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Integrity** | Insight Only | Honesty with oneself and others. |
| **Compassion** | Sympathy Only | Kindness to those in need. |
| **Valor** | Tenacity Only | Courage in the face of danger. |
| **Justice** | Insight + Sympathy | Fair judgment tempered by mercy. |
| **Honor** | Insight + Tenacity | Adherence to one's code and oaths. |
| **Sacrifice** | Sympathy + Tenacity | Giving of oneself for the greater good. |
| **Spirituality** | All Three | The holistic understanding of existence. |
| **Humility** | Independent/None | The grounding principle; knowing one's place. |

## 4. Cities of Veridia
Each city embodies a Tenet:
*   **Aethelgard** (Spirituality): The floating capital, accessible only to the worthy.
*   **Verity** (Integrity): A city of glass and light, where no secrets can be hidden.
*   **Kindred** (Compassion): A village built into the giant Life-Tree.
*   **Ironhold** (Valor): A fortress carved into a volcanic cliff.
*   **Libra** (Justice): The city of high courts and grand libraries.
*   **Oathkeep** (Honor): A disciplined settlement of knights and paladins.
*   **Martyr's Rest** (Sacrifice): A somber, beautiful city near the Void's edge.
*   **Lowell** (Humility): A simple farming community, the starting point for many Paragons.

## 5. The Virtue Swirl
**Location**: The Crossroads of Reality (Accessible from any major Ley-Rift).
**Description**: The only multiverse-franchised bakery in Veridia.
**Proprietor**: *Baker Bob* (A mysterious, jolly inter-dimensional being).
**Specialty**: The **"Swirl Bun"** - A warm, cinnamon-spiced pastry that fully restores Health and Mana.

## 6. The Character: Helga (The Paragon)
*   **Role**: The Protagonist / Archetype.
*   **Appearance**: Strong, Nordic/Fantasy aesthetic. Wears "Fibre-Weave" armor (light but durable).
*   **Backstory**: Summoned from Earth, she was a humble software developer before waking up in the Circle of Stones near Lowell.

## 7. The Art of Magick (Alchemy & Syllables)
Magic in Veridia requires physical catalysts (**Reagents**) and vocal focus (**Syllables**).

### 7.1 Reagents (Catalysts)
To cast spells, Paragons must possess these materials:
*   **Abyssal Pearl** (Black Pearl): Kinetic energy, darkness.
*   **Crimson Lichen** (Blood Moss): Movement, speed.
*   **Sun-Bulb** (Garlic): Warding, protection.
*   **Life Root** (Ginseng): Healing, growth.
*   **Wraith Root** (Mandrake): Raw power, summoning.
*   **Shadow-Leaf** (Nightshade): Illusion, poison.
*   **Weaver's Thread** (Spider Silk): Binding, structure.
*   **Brimstone** (Sulfurous Ash): Fire, energy.

### 7.2 The Syllables of Power
The language of the Ley-Weave:
*   **Gen** (Create)
*   **Des** (Destroy/Lower)
*   **Lux** (Light)
*   **Umbra** (Darkness)
*   **Ignis** (Fire)
*   **Ventus** (Air/Wind)
*   **Vita** (Life/Heal)
*   **Mort** (Death/Harm)
*   **Mot** (Move)
*   **Arc** (Magic)
*   **Mag** (Great/Big)
*   **Min** (Small/Little)

*Example Spells*:
*   *Gen Lux* (Create Light)
*   *Gen Ignis* (Fireball)
*   *Mag Vita* (Great Heal)

## 8. Bestiary & Antagonists
*   **The Discordant**: Twisted creatures born from anti-virtues (e.g., *Gazes* for Falsehood, *Hollows* for Apathy, *Dreads* for Cowardice).
